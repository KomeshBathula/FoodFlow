# FoodFlow Impact Metric Calculations & Formulas

## 1. Beneficiaries Served
$$\text{Beneficiaries} = \text{Redistributed Food (kg)} \times 2.5$$
*Standard assumption based on National Institute of Nutrition (NIN) cooked meal portions (~400g/serving).*

## 2. Greenhouse Gas (CO2e) Avoidance
$$\text{Avoided CO}_2\text{e (kg)} = \text{Redistributed Food (kg)} \times 2.5$$
*Proxy derived from Food and Agriculture Organization (FAO) food-waste carbon footprint benchmark.*

## 3. Financial Savings
$$\text{Cost Saved (INR)} = \text{Redistributed Food (kg)} \times 150$$
*Baseline estimate based on institutional catering input recovery value.*
