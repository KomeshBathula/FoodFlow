# ESG Framework Alignment: Predict → Prevent → Redistribute → Measure → Learn

FoodFlow maps directly to United Nations Sustainable Development Goals:
- **UN SDG 12.3**: Halve per-capita global food waste by 2030.
- **UN SDG 2**: Zero Hunger through rapid local food bank matching.
- **UN SDG 13**: Climate action by preventing organic methane emissions from urban landfills.
