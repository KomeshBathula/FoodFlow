# Release Notes: FoodFlow v1.0.0 (SIH 2026 Prototype)

## Release Summary
FoodFlow v1.0.0 is the official Smart India Hackathon prototype release. It provides a complete, runnable, end-to-end food surplus redistribution platform designed according to the approved product architecture.

## Milestones Achieved
- **Full MERN Foundation**: Node.js/Express backend + React/Vite frontend + MongoDB persistence + Redis cache abstraction.
- **Predictive Intelligence**: Baseline demand forecasting and surplus prediction with explainability and confidence ratings.
- **Decision-Support Quality Scoring**: Combined thermal, shelf-life, and computer-vision heuristics with safety disclaimers.
- **IoT Cold-Chain Telemetry**: Simulated ESP32 hardware streaming with automated anomaly rule triggers.
- **Automated NGO Matching**: Explainable Haversine compatibility scoring.
- **Logistics & Live Tracking**: Turn-by-turn waypoint routing, driver dispatch console, and real-time GPS simulation.
- **ESG & Impact Reporting**: Automated calculation of rescued meals, financial savings, and avoided CO2e greenhouse gas emissions.
- **Tamper-Evident Auditability**: Traceability from initial food preparation to final recipient confirmation.

## Known Prototype Limitations
- AI prediction models and computer-vision classifiers use deterministic heuristic baselines.
- ESP32 telemetry and GPS vehicle movements are simulated.
- Environmental impact factors represent standard FAO proxy coefficients.
